About Machinery Hof

Your reliable partner for technical spare parts, integrated engineering solutions and high-quality raw materials. Founded 2000, based in Bremen, Germany.

Introduction

Who we are

Machinery Hof GmbH is your reliable partner for the supply of technical spare parts, integrated engineering solutions and high-quality raw materials.

With a wide range of products including spare parts for cold milling machines (milling drums, cutting tools), road pavers (screed plates, auger blades), filtration technology (hydraulic, air and fuel filters) and central lubrication systems (lubrication pumps, distributors, lubrication lines), we cover key areas of construction machinery maintenance.

Our company stands out thanks to its extensive storage capacity, the fastest possible logistics and comprehensive quality assurance. We supply both OEM and high-quality aftermarket parts, optimising the maintenance of your machinery.

Mission & Vision

Optimised maintenance, worldwide

We exist to reduce machine downtime for construction equipment operators, fleet managers and distributors, supplying durable, high-quality components alongside expert engineering advice, backed by logistics available round the clock.

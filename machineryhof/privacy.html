We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third parties who assist us in operating our Website and providing services

  • Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize us to share your data

All service providers are bound by data processing agreements and are required to maintain the confidentiality and security of your personal data.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law.

  • Contact information: up to 3 years after last contact unless an ongoing relationship exists

  • Website analytics data: up to 26 months
  • Newsletter subscribers: until unsubscription request

When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including:

  • SSL encryption for data transmission (HTTPS)
  • Secure firewalls and intrusion detection systems
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments

While we strive to protect your personal data, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights Under GDPR

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: You may request a copy of your personal data we hold.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request corrections to inaccurate data.

  • Right to Restrict Processing: You may request limitation of data processing.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request your data in a structured, commonly used format.

Cookie Policy

At present, Machinery HOF GmbH's website machineryhofgmbh.com does not actively use third-party analytics, advertising, or marketing cookies. The current site is not configured to set Google Analytics or other tracking cookies, and there is no active cookie consent banner or preference center in the front-end code.

This Cookie Policy explains the current browser and technical usage on the Website and the way any future cookie use would be managed.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on a device when a website is visited. They may be used to remember preferences, maintain session state, or support website functionality.

At this stage, the current public site does not implement active tracking technologies, and no analytics or marketing cookies are deployed in the source code.

Current cookie usage

  • Technical use only: The site is currently focused on basic website operation and navigation. No active cookie-based analytics or marketing tools are configured.
  • No active analytics cookies: There are no Google Analytics cookies such as _ga, _gid, or _gat implemented in the current frontend code.
  • No active marketing tracking: The Website does not currently use retargeting or advertising cookies.

Browser settings and future changes

If cookies are added in the future for analytics, preference management, or marketing purposes, we will update this policy and implement the necessary consent controls before activation.

Users may still manage browser settings to block or delete cookies at any time. Please note that disabling cookies may affect some website functionality, if such features are introduced later.

Do Not Track

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" feature. The current Website does not respond to Do Not Track signals because there is no active tracking implementation in place at this time.

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy or the Website's current data practices, please contact us at islam@machinery-hof.com.

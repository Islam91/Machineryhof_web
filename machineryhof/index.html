About Machinery Hof

Engineering-grade parts, delivered when your site can't wait

Machinery Hof GmbH is your reliable partner for the supply of technical spare parts, integrated engineering solutions and high‑quality raw materials. Our range covers spare parts for cold milling machines (milling drums, cutting tools), road pavers (screed plates, auger blades), filtration technology (hydraulic, air and fuel filters) and central lubrication systems (lubrication pumps, distributors, lubrication lines) — key areas of construction machinery maintenance.

We stand out through extensive storage capacity, the fastest possible logistics, and comprehensive quality assurance. We supply both OEM and high‑quality aftermarket parts, optimising the maintenance of your machinery from a single source.

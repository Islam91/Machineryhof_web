Equipment We Support

Find the Machinery Hof parts that keep your fleet running, by equipment type.

Equipment types

Excavators

Heavy-duty digging, trenching, and material movement.

Bucket capacity: 0.5–5.0 m³ · Operating weight: 2–50 t

Applications: Digging, Trenching, Loading

Compatible parts: Track Pads, Hydraulic Filters, Lubrication Systems

Bulldozers

Land clearing, pushing soil, and rough leveling.

Blade width: 2.5–4.0 m · Operating weight: 15–85 t

Applications: Clearing, Grading, Pushing

Compatible parts: Track Chains, Sprockets, Fuel Filters

Wheel Loaders

Scooping and loading soil, sand, and aggregates.

Bucket capacity: 0.5–5.0 m³ · Operating weight: 5–50 t

Applications: Loading, Material Handling

Compatible parts: Hydraulic Filters, Air Filters, Lubrication Systems

Dump Trucks & Articulated Haulers

Transporting excavated material and aggregates.

Capacity: 10–50 t · Engine: Diesel

Applications: Material Transport, Hauling

Compatible parts: Fuel Filters, Air Filters

Motor Graders

Precise leveling and shaping before paving.

Blade length: 3.0–4.5 m · Operating weight: 10–20 t

Applications: Grading, Leveling, Road Preparation

Compatible parts: Screed Plates, Auger Blades, Hydraulic Filters
